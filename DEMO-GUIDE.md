# Questbound demo guide

Open Start Game.cmd or the deployed site link.

## Main demonstration

1. Show the Shrey Rawat · BMU opening page and email.
2. Drop `public/assignment-template.txt` into the upload area. Show that all questions, answer keys and levels are generated.
3. Review and click Save assignment & play. The game opens in its own window.
4. Hold Space / J near the lower monster. It falls in two hits.
5. Use the Ladder button to climb. Jump with K / Shift to avoid obstacles.
6. Hit the upper guardian twice. A small Final blow / F prompt appears.
7. Press F. Answer the assignment question. A wrong answer keeps the enemy staggered; a correct answer plays the one-strike sword animation.
8. Walk to or click the gate to reach the next level.
9. In the last room, the same two-hit finisher defeats the boss and rescues the queen.
10. Show honors and export the completed assignment.

For a short boss demonstration, create one question: `2 + 2?`, answer `4`. Every assignment ends in a boss room, including a one-question quest.

## Built-in Sequences & Series answers

1. **19** — 15 + 4.
2. **32** — 5 + (10 - 1) × 3.
3. **210** — 20 × 21 / 2.
4. **486** — 2 × 3^5.
5. **16** — 8 / (1 - 1/2).
6. **93** — 3 + 6 + 12 + 24 + 48.

The first room's lower chest riddle answer is **5**; the upper chest answer is **6**. Each chest gives 25 XP and up to 2 health once only.

Wrong assignment answers prevent The Flawless Crown for that run, but not the main completion honor. Chest mistakes do not affect first-attempt honors. Retry encounter keeps correct answers and claimed treasure.
