Sequences and Series

Q1: Find the next term: 3, 7, 11, 15, ...
Answer: 19
Hint: Add the common difference, 4.

Q2: An AP has first term 5 and common difference 3. Find its 10th term.
Answer: 32
Hint: Use a_n = a + (n - 1)d.

Q3: Find the sum of the first 20 positive integers.
Answer: 210
Hint: Use n(n + 1)/2.
